STYLES

Style in painting refers to the distinctive characteristics and techniques that define an artist's work or a particular art movement. It encompasses elements like brushwork, color palette, composition, and subject matter. Styles can range from realism, which aims to depict subjects accurately, to abstract.

TECHNIQUES

Focus above all on the formal elements of the work, including line, medium, color, light, space, composition, and style. What feeling do they give to the viewer and what are their relationships to the rest of the work as a whole? Consider the context of the work: artist, time, historical background, location.

PATIENCE

Patience in painting is essential for achieving depth and detail in artwork. It involves taking the time to carefully plan each layer, allowing colors to dry, and making thoughtful adjustments. This patience helps artists refine their techniques, observe subtleties in light and form, and fully express their vision.
